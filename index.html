About Pavi Flowers

Pavi Flowers is a small home-based business in New Lynn, West Auckland. Every flower garland and lei is strung by hand using fresh flowers and traditional techniques, and crochet gajra are made by hand too, so your celebration carries a personal touch. I serve customers across Auckland.
